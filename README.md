# Cosmetology Kiosk

## 📌 Project Overview  
#### This app was developed in order to clean the paper clutter in our school's cosmetology shop! It provides easy to access webpages where clients are prompted to input their information.

## 📖 User Guide 
- Sign-In Page: Clients will choose between a Sign-In button and Sign-out. Signing-In will take them to the waiver page, and signing-out will take them to the feedback page.

- Waiver Page: Clients are required to enter their name, signature and date of signature. From this page, they will be directed to the services page.

- Services Page: Choosing from a dropdown what services will be done. If waxing is picked, clients are required to go to the waxing waiver page and complete the form. If not, clients will be directed back to the sign-in page.

- Waxing Waiver Page: A list of questions are meant to be answered (Product usage, allergies, etc). Then, the client will be taken to the feedback.

- Checkout Page: This is where users are taken when the check-out button is clicked. There is a drop down menu of all of the user's that haven't signed out yet. They will choose their own name and then will be led to the feedback form.

- Feedback Page: On this page, user's have 8 questions to answer that range from the attitude of the stylist or professionalism. This is useful for teachers to keep a look out for the behavior of their stylists. 

- Teacher Sign-In Page: This page is strictly for teachers, they will input their school email and password allowing them to access information about clients.

- Teacher Dashboard: Teachers can see information such as client name, service done and their feedback message and rating.
